Jean Bosco Munezero is the founder of Bosco English Academy (BEA), an online English learning platform created to help learners build confidence and improve their English communication skills.

About the Academy

Bosco English Academy provides practical learning opportunities in speaking, listening, reading, writing, grammar and vocabulary. Its mission is to support learners in developing their English proficiency through lessons, practice activities and assessments.

Vision

The vision of Bosco English Academy is to make English learning accessible, practical and engaging while helping learners communicate confidently in different situations.
